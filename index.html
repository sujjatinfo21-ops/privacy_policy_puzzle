Privacy Policy

App: Word Puzzle (com.basenut.puzzle)

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Word Puzzle is a family-friendly, offline-first word search game. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

1. No Personal Information Collected

Word Puzzle does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone data. You can play without creating an account or logging in.

2. Local Device Storage

Your coin balance, category unlock progression, current level, and sound settings are saved strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show ads (interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads for extra hints). Google AdMob may collect non-personal device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud according to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Word Puzzle is safe for all ages. We comply with COPPA guidelines and do not track or profile children.
